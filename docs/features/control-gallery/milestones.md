# Control Gallery delivery stages

The stages are ordered outcomes, not a record of packets, agents, pull requests, or implementation attempts.

1. [Gallery foundation and test harness](milestones/gallery-foundation.md)
2. [Foundations and field controls](milestones/field-controls.md)
3. [Actions, selection, search, and feedback](milestones/interactions-and-feedback.md)
4. [Library quality foundation](milestones/library-quality-foundation.md)
5. [Overlay and navigation controls](milestones/overlays-and-navigation.md)
6. [Cross-control acceptance and release handoff](milestones/acceptance-and-release.md)

The first three stages established the functional baseline. Their release quality is renewed through the library quality foundation rather than by rewriting their history. Overlay and navigation controls remain paused after Popover browser checks and resume only after the quality foundation has approved their internal behavior and visual contracts.
